GITA 2.47 • DEDICATION UNLOCKED

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥

Kid-Friendly Meaning:

"Focus on doing your absolute best in studying and preparing. Don't freeze up by worrying about whether you will get 1st rank. Your job is honest effort; peace follows effort."

STUDENT CHALLENGE

Study 20 minutes without touching your smartphone. Notice your calmness.

MAHABHARATA • ADI PARVA UNLOCKED

द्रोणः पृच्छति: किं पश्यसि? पार्थः उवाच: केवलं पक्षिणः नेत्रं पश्यामि।

Arjuna and the Bird's Eye:

When Guru Drona asked the princes what they saw, others saw the tree, leaves, and sky. Arjuna answered: 'I see only the eye of the toy bird.' That is Ekagrata (singular focus).

STUDENT CHALLENGE

When practicing math or art, mute all background noises and focus on one single problem at a time.

SUDAMA CHARITRA • EQUALITY UNLOCKED

सखा भावं विजानाति न तु वित्तं न वैभवम्।

True Companionship:

Krishna welcomed his poor childhood friend Sudama with open arms, washing his feet with tears of joy. True friendship values character, loyalty, and heart over fancy gadgets or social status.

STUDENT CHALLENGE

Include someone sitting alone during lunchtime at school tomorrow.
